## 1.3.2

- **Map key** - opens the map the way you left it (with the Forever Nodes panel if it was showing), and closes it whatever is at the side.
- **Quest log key** - works like the game's own: shows the quest log in the panel's place, and again closes the map. The panel is back next time you open the map.
- **Quest log arrow** - only slides the quest log in over the panel and back out. Only the Forever Nodes button turns the panel off.
- **Fix** - the panel no longer changes the game's "quest log open" setting, so the map keys and the arrow behave as usual with the panel off.
- **Professions** - ones your character doesn't have can now be opened by default.
